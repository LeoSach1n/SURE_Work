Virtual Physics Laboratory

A suite of four interactive, real-time physics simulations built entirely on the client side using JavaScript and p5.js. Designed to bridge the gap between theoretical mathematics and visual learning.

Rutherford Alpha Scattering

Explore Coulomb's Law of electrostatic repulsion by firing alpha particles at heavy atomic nuclei.

Made by Sachin Tripathi , VNRVJIET , Batch of 28'

J.J. Thomson Cathode Ray Tube

Calculate the e/m ratio by balancing perpendicular electric and magnetic fields to deflect an electron beam.

Made by Sachin Tripathi , VNRVJIET , Batch of 28'

Millikan Oil Drop Experiment

Determine the fundamental charge of an electron by isolating drag, gravity, and electric forces on floating droplets.

Made by Sachin Tripathi , VNRVJIET , Batch of 28'

Solid Scattering

Deduce hidden solid shapes using rigid-body elastic collision reflections and cartesian impact angles.

Made by Sachin Tripathi , VNRVJIET , Batch of 28'
